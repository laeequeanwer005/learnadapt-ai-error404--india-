02 / OUR APPROACH

A learning path that
updates with evidence.

Five steps, one transparent loop. No mysterious black-box recommendation — the learner can see what changed and why.

STEP 01 / 05

OBSERVE

Collect skill-tagged attempts and practice outcomes.

STEP 02 / 05

PREDICT

Estimate P(next attempt is correct) with uncertainty in view.

STEP 03 / 05

CHECK

Apply explicit prerequisite and evidence-sufficiency gates.

STEP 04 / 05

RANK

Balance skill gap, challenge, and review urgency.

STEP 05 / 05

ADAPT

Refresh the evidence and reassess after each attempt.

06 / RESPONSIBLE AI

Helpful guidance.
Never a permanent label.

The point isn't to classify students forever. It's to find a useful next step today — and revise that guidance when evidence changes.

Responsible by design

✓
Privacy-aware demonstration

Every visible learner and attempt is synthetic. The local demo does not send data anywhere.

✓
Low-evidence honesty

New learners enter diagnostic mode instead of receiving a high-confidence guess.

✓
Agency over automation

Show reasons, surface limitations, and leave space for educator or learner override in the full product.

Potential value

↗
More relevant practice

Use observed skill gaps to focus the next activity instead of repeating the same lesson for everyone.

↗
Clearer gap awareness

Help students and teachers understand which evidence changed a recommendation.

↗
Adaptation without stigma

Today's recommendation is revisable, not a permanent judgment of ability.
